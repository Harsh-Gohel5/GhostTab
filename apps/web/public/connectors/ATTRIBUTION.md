# Connector brand assets

These SVG marks identify their respective products in GhostTab's integration
catalog. They do not imply endorsement, partnership or integration availability.
Brand names and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

`SOURCES.json` records the source of each of the 49 locally bundled files.
No remote logo service is contacted when the gallery renders.

- SVG Logos / gilbarbara: CC0-1.0, https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos
- Simple Icons: CC0-1.0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
  Monochrome paths use the product's brand color for display.
- Dashboard Icons: original license included in `DASHBOARD-ICONS-LICENSE`,
  https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons. Microsoft product marks retain
  their owners' rights. Outlook Mail and Outlook Calendar use the Outlook mark.
- Microsoft Planner and ServiceNow: source SVGs from Wikimedia Commons; the
  linked file pages describe the original brand authorship and applicable notices.
- Freshdesk: the product mark served by Freshworks' own website.
- FigJam: the inline product mark from Figma's own FigJam navigation, extracted
  without changing its paths or colors.

SVG containers may be normalized for standalone local display. This collection
adds presentation assets only; it adds no provider permissions or network access.
